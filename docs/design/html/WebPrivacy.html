Who we are

Furkin is made by Pixel Witch, a sole proprietorship in The Hague, the Netherlands. Pixel Witch is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: hello@furkin.app.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, email address and an optional photo.
  • Your Household: its name, members and their roles.
  • Your pets: profiles, routines, health records, weights, documents, contacts and photos you add.
  • Activity logs: what was done, for which pet, by whom and when.
  • Push notification tokens for your devices.
  • Website: the email you give us for the waitlist.

Why we use it

[To run the app for you and your Household, to send the reminders you turn on, and to email you once when Furkin launches if you joined the waitlist. Legal bases to be confirmed in review.]
